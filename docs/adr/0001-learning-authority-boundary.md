# ADR 0001: Learning-management authority boundary

## Status

Proposed

## Decision

The platform owns learning offerings, enrollment, learner affiliations, progression projections, and versioned completion decisions. It treats employment linkage, authored content, observed learning statements, assessment responses, and commercial payment state as external authorities.

## Consequences

Non-employee learners are supported without synthetic HR records. Completion decisions are reproducible from exact policy and evidence versions. Integrations remain replaceable behind versioned contracts.
